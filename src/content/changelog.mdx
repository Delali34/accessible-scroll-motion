## v0.1.0

October 22, 2026

The first published version of this documentation.

- A [quickstart](/quickstart) that builds a scroll-triggered fade-up with GSAP,
  ScrollTrigger, and `useGSAP()` in a fresh Next.js 16 app.
- A [guide to adding a Reduce motion toggle](/guides/reduce-motion-toggle),
  including troubleshooting for the errors you're most likely to see.
- A [reference](/reference/motion-helpers) for `motionAllowed()`, `fadeUp()`,
  and `settleAnimations()`.
